02 // PICK A FARM, THEN SEE ITS HISTORY

Choose one of the farms listed under the map, click a field on the satellite map, or press "Draw my land" to outline your own.

You will then see the weather and soil of that land for every season since 2006, and what the climate is projected to do next. Nothing is shown until you pick land, because a town centre or a point with no field tells you nothing about farming.
